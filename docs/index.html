Your YouTube channel's assistant, running on your own computer.

Loopcast is a desktop app for YouTube creators. It watches the folder you export videos to, prepares titles, descriptions and tags from your channel's own niche, and schedules uploads and posts. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

What Loopcast does

Optimize your videos. When a new video lands in your folder, Loopcast suggests titles, a description and tags based on your channel's topics and public keyword research.
Approval queue. Every upload and post waits in a queue on your computer until you approve it. You can switch this off per type in Settings.
Uploads to your channel. When you approve a video, Loopcast uploads it to your YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, as Private with the publish time you chose, plus the thumbnail you picked.
Learns your niche. After you connect YouTube, Loopcast reads your channel description and your videos' titles, tags and view counts to work out your topics, so suggestions match what you actually make.

How Loopcast uses your Google and YouTube data

Loopcast uses YouTube API Services. You sign in with your own Google account. Your sign-in tokens are kept in your computer's own credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain or the Linux Secret Service), and the data Loopcast reads from YouTube is stored only on your computer. Loopcast has no servers that store your data, never sells it, and doesn't use it for advertising. You can disconnect at any time in Loopcast, or revoke access in your Google Account settings.
